How it works

  1. You write itYour letter gets encrypted right in your browser, to a future round of drand, a randomness network run by universities and companies around the world. The key for that round doesn't exist yet, so nobody can open it early.
  2. It waits on PolygonThe locked letter sits in the contract and you get the envelope as an NFT. It draws itself on chain, no server behind it.
  3. The day comesdrand publishes the key, anyone can open it, and once the text goes back on chain the envelope turns into the letter for good.
