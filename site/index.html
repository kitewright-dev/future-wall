How it works

Your letter is encrypted in your browser to a future round of drand, a randomness network run by universities and companies around the world. The key for that round simply doesn't exist yet, so there is nothing anyone can do to open it early.

The encrypted letter is stored in the contract on Polygon. Once the date passes the key gets published, anyone can open it, and whoever puts the text back on chain turns the envelope into the letter itself.

First 1000 letters get a gold Genesis seal. Letters set for 1 January get a blue New Year seal.
